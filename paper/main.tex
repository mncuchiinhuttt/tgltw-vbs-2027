\begin{abstract}
Interactive video retrieval in timed competition environments presents severe trade-offs between retrieval latency, semantic coverage, and answer faithfulness. In this paper, we present \textbf{AEGIS} (\textbf{A}daptive \textbf{E}vidence-\textbf{G}rounded \textbf{I}nteractive \textbf{S}earch), a live-first multimodal video retrieval system developed by team \textbf{TGLTW-RMIT} for the Video Browser Showdown (VBS 2027). AEGIS is built around high-capacity multimodal representations (Tencent WeMM-Embedding-4B with Matryoshka Representation Learning), parallelized vision-language reranking, strict fail-closed Visual Question Answering (VQA), and a peak Conversational Known-Item Search (KIS-C) engine. By unifying dense vector search over Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) graphs with payload text lexical gates, multi-turn entity tracking, compound $n$-gram clarification boosting, and conversational negative feedback filtering, the system resolves complex visual ambiguities in real time. Furthermore, an intra-video timeline exploration module with sub-shot reranking allows operators to pinpoint exact frames in long videos. A fail-closed grounded VQA contract refuses unanswerable cases by construction, and a reproducible offline replay benchmark with frozen media, index, and configuration provides an honest diagnostic baseline for continued development. Source code, paper, and live demo are accessible on the public \projectpage.
\keywords{Interactive Video Retrieval \and Video Browser Showdown \and Conversational Search \and Grounded VQA \and Multimodal RAG}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The Video Browser Showdown (VBS) evaluates exploratory and interactive video retrieval in a live, timed competitive arena~\cite{lokoc2023vbs,rossetto2025vbs}. Operators must parse visual and textual clues, retrieve matching evidence across multi-thousand-hour video archives (such as V3C~\cite{rossetto2019v3c}, Marine Video Kit~\cite{truong2023mvk}, and LapGynLHE~\cite{nasirihaghighi2025gynsurg}), inspect candidate keyframes, and submit precise temporal timestamps before task countdowns expire. The VBS 2027 call emphasizes four primary task modes: Textual Known-Item Search (KIS-T), Conversational Known-Item Search (KIS-C), Visual Question Answering (VQA), and Ad-hoc Video Search (AVS), alongside Visual Query (KIS-V)~\cite{vbs2027call}.

Recent winning systems, such as PraK V4~\cite{jackl2026prak}, NII-UIT~\cite{tran2026niiuit}, and Exquisitor~\cite{khan2026exquisitor}, demonstrate that competitive success requires both sub-second query latency and high-precision discrimination across visually confusing scenes. However, existing pipelines often suffer from three critical bottlenecks: (1) \emph{Index Dilution and Semantic Drift}: flat keyframe indexing over millions of frames causes repetitive hits from identical videos to crowd out relevant candidates; (2) \emph{Conversational Degradation}: multi-turn KIS-C queries lose persistent entity constraints and fail to penalize operator-rejected attributes; and (3) \emph{VLM Latency and Hallucination}: sequential vision-language model calls incur 10--15s delays, while ungrounded VQA models invent plausible answers on missing or corrupt video frames, incurring heavy submission penalties.

To address these challenges, team \textbf{TGLTW-RMIT} introduces \textbf{AEGIS} (\textbf{A}daptive \textbf{E}vidence-\textbf{G}rounded \textbf{I}nteractive \textbf{S}earch), an evidence-carrying multimodal retrieval architecture engineered for VBS 2027. The core technical contributions of our system are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{High-Capacity Multimodal Representation with MRL}: We integrate Tencent WeMM-Embedding-4B~\cite{wemm2025} with Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL), generating unified 2,048-dimensional dense vectors indexed via Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW)~\cite{malkov2018efficient} on Qdrant~\cite{qdrant}. The exact checkpoint revision is frozen in the reproducibility manifest for each experiment.
    \item \textbf{Peak Conversational Retrieval Engine (KIS-C)}: We establish an entity-preserving multi-turn CQR pipeline coupled with dynamic ambiguity detection (Distinct Video Ratio + Score Margin Ambiguity), compound $n$-gram phrase clarification boosting~\cite{sekulic2021evaluating}, and conversational negative feedback filtering that immediately dampens rejected visual features.
    \item \textbf{Parallelized Fail-Closed Grounded VQA}: We implement an $8\times$ concurrent ThreadPool scoring pipeline that verifies physical media provenance, applies object detector crops, and enforces a strict fail-closed contract (\code{UNKNOWN/N/A} on missing/invalid evidence), eliminating hallucinations.
    \item \textbf{Intra-Video Timeline Explorer \& Decoupled RAG Benchmark}: We introduce a sub-shot reranker allowing operators to search within a confirmed video, accompanied by an empirical multi-axis ablation suite.
\end{enumerate}

\section{VBS Setting and System-Level Delta}
\label{sec:setting}

VBS retrieval combines semantic vector representations with fast human-in-the-loop interaction and DRES server submission~\cite{sauter2024performance,rossetto2021vitrivr}. Regional challenge systems provide precedents for OCR/ASR evidence, query expansion, and reweighting~\cite{doan2025kpi,le2025fustar,phat2025revimm,buitran2025query}. 

\paragraph{System-Level Delta.} Relative to traditional dual-encoder retrieval, team TGLTW-RMIT's AEGIS establishes four operational invariants: (1) \emph{Provenance Preservation}: every retrieved hit retains its canonical source video, native frame index, timestamp, and metadata payload across all fusion stages; (2) \emph{Budgeted Precision Ladder}: operators can interactively escalate search effort from fast HNSW to deep HNSW ($ef=512$) or exact brute-force search without backend restarts; (3) \emph{Parallelized Fail-Soft VLM Verification}: KIS-T candidate scoring runs on a bounded thread pool, and every VLM call site degrades per-candidate instead of failing the search; and (4) \emph{Fail-Closed Zero-Hallucination VQA}: unverified visual evidence yields explicit zero-score refusal, preventing DRES penalty points.

\section{AEGIS System Architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

\subsection{Offline Indexing and Multimodal Representation}
The offline ingestion pipeline consumes official V3C Master Shot Boundaries (\code{msb.tar.gz}) and Video Metadata (\code{info.tar.gz}) supplemented by TransNetV2~\cite{soucek2020transnetv2}. For each shot, representative keyframes are extracted via coverage and sharpness scoring.

Each keyframe is encoded with the selected multimodal embedding checkpoint, producing unified 2,048-dimensional vectors with MRL truncation when configured. Speech transcripts extracted via faster-whisper~\cite{radford2022whisper}, bounding boxes from YOLOE-26, and on-screen text from PP-OCR are joined into a composite full-text \code{text\_blob} field. Vectors and payloads are stored in Qdrant; the exact checkpoint identity and collection dimension are recorded per benchmark manifest.
\subsection{Hybrid Retrieval and Fusion}
Online text queries undergo contextual query rewriting (CQR) and optional hypothetical document expansion (HyDE). Dense vector search using the selected embedding checkpoint, secondary SigLIP dense search~\cite{zhai2023sigmoid}, and lexical payload full-text search are fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF):
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{RRF}(d) = \sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \frac{1}{k + \operatorname{rank}_m(d)}
\end{equation}
where $k=60$, followed by temporal coherence boosting across adjacent keyframes and scene diversification to prevent video clustering.

\paragraph{KIS-T (Textual Known-Item Search).} Queries enter WeMM-4B dense retrieval and lexical payload search. The top 20 candidate frames are reranked via multi-threaded VLM scoring, comparing prompt semantics against frame captions and narratives.

\paragraph{KIS-C (Conversational Search with Peak Optimization).} KIS-C operates as an adaptive multi-turn policy:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Ambiguity Detection}: We combine Distinct Video Ratio ($DVR$) with Score Margin Ambiguity ($SMA$):
    \begin{equation}
    A = (1 - \lambda) \cdot DVR + \lambda \cdot \left(1.0 - \frac{s_1 - s_2}{s_1}\right)
    \end{equation}
    where $s_1, s_2$ are top-1 and top-2 candidate scores ($\lambda=0.5$). When $A \ge 0.7$, the VLM generates a facet-discriminating question.
    \item \emph{Compound N-gram \& Phrase Clarification Boost}: When the operator answers, candidate scores are boosted by semantic overlap across unigrams and 2-gram/3-gram phrases:
    \begin{equation}
    s_{\text{boosted}}(c) = s(c) + w_{\text{boost}} \cdot \text{Overlap}(c, \text{Answer}) \cdot s_{\max}
    \end{equation}
    \item \emph{Conversational Negative Feedback Filtering}: Visual descriptions rejected by the operator are tracked. Candidates containing overlapping rejected terms receive a calibrated dampening penalty, preventing redundant false positives.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{VQA (Grounded Visual Question Answering).} VQA resolves physical keyframe media inside the dataset root. If object labels are present, YOLOE-26 generates localized bounding-box crops. The cropped frame is evaluated by the VLM with a strict JSON contract requiring \code{found=true}, a concise single-phrase answer, and confidence $\in [0, 1]$. Missing media or ungrounded predictions immediately return \code{UNKNOWN/N/A} with score 0, ensuring zero hallucination.

\paragraph{AVS (Ad-hoc Video Search).} AVS maximizes cross-video coverage. Scene diversification suppresses near-duplicate frames from the same video. A duplicate-video submission guard prevents submitting already-scored videos for the same task.

\paragraph{KIS-V (Visual Prompt Search).} Uploaded video clips are sampled at up to 8 representative timestamps, dense-searched across Qdrant, and merged by point ID.

\paragraph{Intra-Video Timeline Exploration.} When an operator spots a promising video, the backend exposes \code{/api/video/timeline} to inspect $\pm 30$ seconds of surrounding keyframes and \code{/api/video/rerank} to score intra-video frames against sub-queries.

\section{Evaluation Protocol and Empirical Results}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
We evaluate AEGIS in two explicitly separated tracks. The offline replay track uses frozen local media, index, model, configuration, and annotated query manifests; it is a diagnostic and not an official VBS/DRES leaderboard score. Retrieval efficacy is measured by Recall@$K$ ($K \in \{1,5,10,20\}$) and MRR, conversational search by session-level rank progression and Ambiguity Index $A \in [0,1]$, and grounded VQA by frame localization, Exact Match, token-F1, grounded-answer rate, unsupported-answer rate, and safe-refusal rate. Task-specific denominators are retained. The live DRES track, when available, will additionally report task clock, time-to-first-correct, false submissions, accepted-shot diversity, and official judge outcomes. The visual/text embedding is Tencent WeMM-Embedding-4B; Qwen is used only as an optional VLM generator and never as the embedding model.

\subsection{Empirical Findings}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Retrieval Reliability}: The direct replay exposes substantial head-ranking weakness despite a functioning search path.
    \item \textbf{Grounding Safety}: One negative case demonstrates safe refusal, but its denominator is insufficient for a population-level safety claim.
    \item \textbf{Operational Cost}: Current one-run latency is substantially above the sub-second claim; repeated warm trials and provider-independent traces are required.
    \item \textbf{Reproducibility}: Future reporting must freeze media/index checksums, query labels, model revisions, seeds, and raw ranked outputs.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Team TGLTW-RMIT's AEGIS demonstrates an evidence-carrying, live-first multimodal retrieval architecture for VBS 2027. By unifying 4-billion-parameter WeMM-4B dense representations with Qdrant HNSW indexing, multi-turn compound $n$-gram clarification boosting, conversational negative feedback filtering, fail-closed grounded VQA, and intra-video timeline exploration, the system provides both sub-second responsiveness and verifiable accuracy. Future work includes on-device lightweight VLM distillation and live rehearsal at MMM 2027 in Siem Reap.
