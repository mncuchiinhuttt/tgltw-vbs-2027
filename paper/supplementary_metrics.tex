\section*{Purpose}
This supplementary note defines every metric used in the AEGIS evaluation and ablation tables. Metrics are reported at the query, candidate, answer, or system level as indicated below.

Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) & Query / retrieval & Mean of the reciprocal rank of the first relevant result: $\mathrm{MRR}=N^{-1}\sum_i 1/r_i$. Higher is better; it rewards earlier correct candidates. \\
RRF score & Candidate / fusion & Weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion score used to combine dense, lexical, and secondary-encoder rankings: $\mathrm{RRF}(d)=\sum_m w_m/(k+\mathrm{rank}_m(d))$. \\
VQA Exact Match & Answer / VQA & Percentage of questions for which the normalized generated answer exactly matches the reference answer. \\
VQA faithfulness & Answer / VQA & Percentage of answers supported by the resolved media evidence and the requested visual content. \\
Hallucination rate & Answer / VQA & Fraction of answers unsupported by valid media evidence or contradicted by the evidence. A fail-closed refusal is not counted as a hallucination. \\
Ambiguity score & Query / KIS-C & Combined ambiguity signal from Distinct Video Ratio (DVR) and Score Margin Ambiguity (SMA). Lower values indicate stronger convergence after clarification. \\
Ambiguity reduction & Query / KIS-C & Difference between the initial and final ambiguity scores: $A_{\mathrm{initial}}-A_{\mathrm{final}}$. Higher reduction indicates more effective conversational disambiguation. \\

Throughput (QPS) & System / concurrency & Completed query or candidate-scoring operations per second under the stated worker count. \\
Recall versus exact & Retrieval / precision ladder & Recall of an approximate HNSW mode relative to the exact brute-force ranking over the same collection. \\
Overall RAG score & Benchmark / corpus & Aggregate benchmark score combining retrieval quality, grounded answer quality, conversational behavior, and operational measurements under the declared evaluation protocol. \\

\section*{Reporting conventions}
All percentages are reported on the evaluated query corpus and collection snapshot. Latency values include only the stages named in the corresponding experiment. Approximate HNSW results and exact brute-force results are compared using the same query vectors and candidate collection. For VQA, missing or invalid provenance produces the explicit fail-closed response \texttt{UNKNOWN/N/A} with zero confidence rather than an unsupported answer.
